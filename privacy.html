Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

This policy explains how PNK Notes ("the app"), developed by Abdul Halim, handles your information. In short: your notes stay on your device and, only if you turn on sync, in a private folder in your own Google Drive. The developer never receives your notes or your Google data.

1. Information the app stores on your device

  • Your notes (titles, text, formatting, favorites, trash state and timestamps) and your settings are stored in a local database on your device.
  • On Android, the app's data is excluded from device backup and device-to-device transfer.
  • Notes in the trash are deleted permanently after 30 days, or earlier if you delete them yourself.

2. Google user data (only if you connect Google Drive)

Google Drive sync is optional. If you choose Connect Google Drive, the app signs you in with Google and requests one permission (scope):

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata – read and write the app's own hidden application-data folder in your Google Drive.

With this permission, the app accesses the following Google user data:

  • Files in the app's hidden application-data folder, which contain copies of your notes. The app creates, reads, updates and deletes these files to keep your notes the same on all your devices. The app cannot see or access any other file in your Google Drive.
  • Basic account information (your email address, display name and a Google account identifier). This is used only to show which account is connected and to make sure notes sync with the right account.
  • Sign-in tokens issued by Google. They are stored only on your device, in the operating system's secure credential storage, and are used only to access the folder described above.

3. How your data is shared

Your notes and Google user data are transferred only between your device and Google's servers (Google sign-in and the Google Drive API). There is no PNK Notes server. The app does not sell, rent, share or transfer your data to the developer or to any third party, does not show ads, and does not include analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.

If you use the app's Share action, the note you choose is handed to the app you pick in your device's share menu. This happens only when you start it.

4. Limited Use disclosure

PNK Notes' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the app's note sync feature, is never used for advertising, and is never read by humans.

5. Security

All communication with Google uses encrypted HTTPS connections. Sign-in tokens are kept in the operating system's secure storage. On Windows, the browser sign-in uses Google's recommended loopback flow with PKCE.

6. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Disconnect: in the app, open Settings → Google Drive → Disconnect. This revokes the app's access to your Google account and removes the stored sign-in from your device.
  • Revoke access from Google: visit myaccount.google.com/connections and remove PNK Notes.
  • Delete synced notes from Google Drive: in Google Drive on the web, open Settings → Manage apps → PNK Notes → Options → Delete hidden app data.
  • Delete notes on a device: delete them in the app, or uninstall the app to remove all of its local data.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
