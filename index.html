PNK Notes

A simple, offline-first note keeper for Android and Windows, with optional sync through a private folder in your own Google Drive.

What it does

  • Write notes in a large, distraction-free editor with basic formatting.
  • Notes save automatically as you type and work fully offline.
  • Search, favorites, and a trash with restore (emptied automatically after 30 days).
  • Optional Google Drive sync keeps your notes the same on your Android phone and Windows PC.

How Google Drive sync works

Sync is optional and off until you choose Connect Google Drive in Settings.

PNK Notes asks Google for one permission only: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata. It lets the app read and write its own hidden application-data folder in your Google Drive, and nothing else. PNK Notes cannot see, open or change any of your other Drive files.

Your notes go directly between your device and your own Google Drive. There is no PNK Notes server, and your notes are never sent to the developer or anyone else.

You can disconnect at any time in Settings. This revokes the app's access to your Google account.
